Made for little hands

Bubble Bunny is a gentle learning garden for ages 3–5. There is no timer, no way to lose, and no reading required.

  • Each bubble holds a colourful shape with a number inside.
  • Follow the spoken prompt to find a colour, shape, or number.
  • Every correct bubble earns a star and a happy cheer.
  • A wrong tap never loses a star — the bunny simply says “try again”.
  • Tap the grass to send the bunny hopping anywhere you like.

No accounts, ads, purchases, or data collection. Voice prompts use your device's own speech engine and stay on this device.
